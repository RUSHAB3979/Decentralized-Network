# Legal Starter Document (India) for This Startup

**Context**: P2P compute + cloud-gaming marketplace (`orchestrator` + `provider-agent` platform).

**Important**: This is not legal advice. Use this as a founder working draft, then have an Indian startup counsel/company secretary finalize it.

## 1. The one legal document to finalize first

Before product scaling, funding, or team hiring, the most critical document is a **Founders' Agreement** (signed among all co-founders).

Why first:

- Prevents equity and role disputes.
- Protects startup IP from day one.
- Creates investor-ready governance basics.

---

## 2. Founders' Agreement (working template)

Use the following as a draft structure for counsel review.

### 2.1 Parties and date

- Founders: `[Founder 1 legal name]`, `[Founder 2 legal name]`, ...
- Effective date: `[DD-MMM-YYYY]`

### 2.2 Startup purpose

- Business: decentralized marketplace for cloud gaming sessions and compute jobs.
- Planned entity type: `[Private Limited Company / LLP]` in India.

### 2.3 Founder roles and commitments

- Founder A role: `[CEO/Product/Growth]`
- Founder B role: `[CTO/Engineering]`
- Founder C role: `[Ops/Finance/etc.]`
- Minimum time commitment: `[full-time / defined hours]`
- Decision authority for each function.

### 2.4 Equity split and vesting

- Initial equity split: `[A% / B% / C%]`
- Vesting schedule: `[e.g., 4 years with 1-year cliff]`
- Unvested equity treatment on exit.
- Future ESOP pool target: `[e.g., 10%-15%]`

### 2.5 IP ownership and assignment

- All code, product artifacts, branding, docs, and inventions created by founders for the startup are assigned to the company upon incorporation.
- Founders must execute confirmatory IP assignment deeds when requested.
- No founder may reuse third-party code/content without proper license compliance.

### 2.6 Confidentiality

- Confidential information includes roadmap, algorithms, user/provider data, financials, contracts, and security controls.
- Confidentiality obligations survive founder exit.

### 2.7 Compensation and expense policy

- Founder salaries (if any): `[amount/phase triggers]`
- Reimbursement policy and approval thresholds.

### 2.8 Governance and voting

- Routine matters: simple majority.
- Reserved matters (equity issuance, debt, acquisition, dissolution, major pivots): supermajority/unanimous.
- Board composition target after incorporation.

### 2.9 Founder exit / bad leaver / good leaver

- Resignation, termination, incapacity, death scenarios.
- Repurchase rights for unvested shares.
- Treatment of vested shares by good-leaver vs bad-leaver definitions.

### 2.10 Restrictions

- Non-solicit (employees/customers/providers) for `[12-24 months]`.
- Narrow confidentiality and IP protections.
- Note: post-termination non-compete enforceability in India is limited; counsel should tailor this clause.

### 2.11 Dispute resolution

- Governing law: India.
- Dispute process: negotiation -> mediation -> arbitration.
- Arbitration seat/venue: `[City, India]`.

### 2.12 Conversion on incorporation

- This agreement binds founders immediately.
- It is to be mirrored into company constitutional + shareholder documentation at incorporation.

## 3. Legal document stack to complete next (priority order)

1. **Incorporation set** (MCA): SPICe+, MoA, AoA, linked filings.

## 5. Startup-specific legal risks for this product

- **Data handling risk**: user + provider telemetry and potential personal data.
- **Platform liability risk**: content/usage misuse and prohibited workloads.
- **IP contamination risk**: unlicensed model/code/artifacts in submitted jobs.
- **Provider misuse risk**: unauthorized compute, fraud, or abuse of credits/payouts.

Mitigation baseline:

- Strict Acceptable Use Policy.
- Clear provider/user suspension rights in ToS.
- Audit logging and incident response process.
- Strong IP warranty + indemnity clauses in provider/customer terms.
